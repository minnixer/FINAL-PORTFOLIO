Project Description

Patchworks is a reimagined model of supportive hosuing for youth who age out of the foster care system. After youth turn 18 in the system, they are faced with signifacant risk, some of which include substance dependancy, incarceration, and homelessness. This housing facility is a new look of what transitional hosuing looks like as it will include various services such as job resource center, mental health sservices, and life skilsl workshops. WHile integrating Trauma-Informed Design (TID), this proejct shows how the environemnts around us can dramatically efect our wellness and motivation.
